Why I Built All of This

The honest version.

// the story

From the SIP ladder to the infrastructure around it

I needed something scalable and industry-grade that I could place in any cloud — or bare metal. The lab lets me play with real equipment and break things without the pressure of the job. I can simulate calls, test failover, work on deployment strategies like blue/green, and apply what I'm learning directly to my field.

Courses are good but you don't get to apply them to your industry. Now I do. I want to build VoIP architecture using DevOps methodologies — and next I'm going into DevSecOps. This is a journey of discovery and I'm documenting every step of it.

// what I'm actually good at

Operations first

I find issues. I read logs. I troubleshoot SIP and read call ladders. I solve problems — sometimes in ways nobody else thought to try. The DevOps skills are real and growing, but they exist to make the voice infrastructure better. Not the other way around.

// the differentiator

Both sides of the wall

Most DevOps engineers can't read a SIP trace. Most VoIP engineers can't build a Terraform module or write an Ansible role. I can do both — and I've shipped the infrastructure to prove it across 4 clouds simultaneously.
